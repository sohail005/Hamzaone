Your Ideas. Made Real.

Premium e-commerce for 3D printed goods in India: ready-made products, made-to-order products and custom prints from uploaded STL/3MF files, paid through Razorpay and tracked from print bed to doorstep.

Goals
  • Fast product discovery and low-friction guest checkout
  • Clear split between production time and shipping time
  • An understandable request, quote, pay, track sequence for custom prints
  • An admin tool tuned for daily print operations
Brand personality

Innovative, premium, modern, trustworthy, minimal, creative, technically sophisticated and approachable. Warm paper ground, ink text, one deliberate orange.

Design principles
  1. The object is the hero
  2. Say how long it takes, honestly
  3. Orange only where a decision happens
  4. Every state has a way forward
